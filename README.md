# CertLedger

CertLedger is an experimental legal-record system built around independently verifiable digital artifacts, durable identifiers, and reproducible provenance.

The project began as a workspace for managing evidence and is developing toward a broader question: what happens when ordinary people can create legal records whose existence, version, references, and publication history can be independently checked without relying entirely on the institution holding the record?

## Important boundary

A permanent ledger record is not automatically true, authenticated, admissible, or legally authoritative. CertLedger is intended to make specific technical facts easier to verify and to make the remaining legal questions more explicit.
